Why ShareGate

Security built into every layer

Files never leave your browser unencrypted. Access is enforced onchain, so you stay in control at all times.

End-to-End Encryption

Files are encrypted in your browser with AES-256 before they ever touch the network.

Decentralized Storage

Encrypted files are stored on IPFS, distributed and resilient by design.

Wallet-Gated Access

Only the wallets you approve can decrypt and download a shared file.

Revoke Anytime

Instantly revoke access onchain, directly from your dashboard, in a single transaction.
